About Kelong Edwin Cheruiyot

Kelong Edwin Cheruiyot is a Kenyan educator specializing in Chemistry and Mathematics. He holds a Bachelor of Education (Science) degree from Moi University, completed in 2025.


His academic and professional interests include Chemistry, Mathematics education, teaching, mentorship, personal development and youth empowerment.


Kelong Edwin is passionate about helping learners understand scientific and mathematical concepts, develop critical thinking skills and build confidence through education.


Through teaching and continuous professional development, he aims to contribute positively to education and inspire young people to pursue knowledge, discipline and personal growth.
